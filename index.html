Event Overview

The Shire Cup is a 36-hole Ryder Cup style golf competition featuring team Hog vs team Microdick competing in six different formats:
  • 2v2 Scramble
  • 2v2 Alternate Shot
  • 2v2 Scotch
  • 2v2 Bramble
  • 2v2 Low / Combined
  • Individual Matches

Competition Formats

Scramble

4 Matches

6 Holes

Both players hit each shot. The team selects the best ball and both players play from that spot until holed.

Alternate Shot

4 Matches

6 Holes

Partners alternate hitting the same ball until the hole is completed.

Scotch

4 Matches

6 Holes

Both players tee off, select a drive, then alternate shots with one ball until holed.

Bramble

4 Matches

6 Holes

Both players hit drives, choose the best drive, then each player plays their own ball into the hole.

Low / Combined

4 Matches

6 Holes

The lowest individual score and the combined team score are both compared each hole. 1 pt awarded for lowest score, 1 pt for lowest team total. Team can get .5, 1, 1.5 or 2pts per hole

Individuals

8 Matches

6 Holes

Head-to-head singles match play with one point awarded to the winner.

How Points Are Won

Each match is worth 1 point. If a match is tied after 6 holes, each team receives 0.5 points. A total of 28 points are available during the competition, and the team with the most points wins the Shire Cup. IN THE EVENT OF A 14-14 TIE, a sudden death 4 v 4 scramble will take place with 4 players chosen by each captain.
