Event Overview

The Shire Cup is a 36-hole Ryder Cup style golf competition featuring two teams of eight players competing in six different formats:
  • 2v2 Scramble
  • 2v2 Alternate Shot
  • 2v2 Scotch
  • 2v2 Bramble
  • 2v2 Low / Combined
  • Individual Matches
