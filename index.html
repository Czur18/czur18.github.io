Event Overview

The Shire Cup is a 36-hole Ryder Cup style golf competition featuring team Hog vs team Microdick competing in six different formats:
  • 2v2 Scramble
  • 2v2 Alternate Shot
  • 2v2 Scotch
  • 2v2 Bramble
  • 2v2 Low / Combined
  • Individual Matches
2026 Captains Match Recap
The 2026 Shire Cup captains match between Hog Downer and Microdick Nick was one for the ages. On a cold Saturday morning at the old Cranwell Resort, now known as Wyndhurst Golf Club, the two captains teed it up in what would become an instant classic. The match began with matching pars on the opening hole after Nick stuffed a wedge to eight feet but failed to convert the birdie putt. Things quickly turned ugly on the second hole as both players stumbled to sevens, sending the match to the third hole all square. Disaster struck for Nick on the third after rolling his tee shot and then blasting his third shot into the woods, allowing Hog to take a 1-up lead. Another poor tee shot on four helped Hog build a 2-up advantage. The fifth appeared to be an opportunity for Nick to stop the bleeding after Downer chunked one off the tee while Nick found the green. What followed was nothing short of shocking as Nick four-putted, handing another hole to Hog and falling behind 3 down. Nick finally responded with a gritty par on six to trim the deficit to two. However, Hog was far from finished. Consecutive pars on seven and eight extended the lead to four holes and appeared to put the match out of reach. Nick clawed one back on nine after Downer found himself trapped in the trees, sending the match to the back side with Hog holding a commanding 3-up lead. Then came the controversy. As the pair made the turn, eight groups slipped Big E a dollar and skipped ahead to the back nine, forcing the captains to bypass the tenth and eleventh holes entirely and jump directly to the par-three twelfth. The momentum immediately shifted. Nick won the twelfth to cut the lead to two and then stared down a crucial ten-footer on thirteen. After Hog holed a clutch putt, Nick answered by rolling his attempt straight into the center of the cup to maintain the two-hole deficit. The pivotal moment came at the notorious fourteenth, known as "Pond Jump." After finding the bunker, Nick produced one of the best shots of the day from wet sand before pouring in an eight-footer to cut the lead to one. Standing on the next tee, Nick famously declared, "I've nicked the Hog's main artery." Moments later, Hog sprayed a tee shot toward the trees but received a lucky kick and managed to stay alive in the hole with a brilliant punch shot and pitch. Unfortunately for Downer, Nick nearly holed his approach for a kick-in birdie, completely erasing what had once been a four-hole deficit. Matching bogeys on sixteen left the match all square. The comeback was complete. On the next hole, a lost ball from Hog allowed Nick to take his first lead of the day at 1 up with three to play. Nick then struck another beautiful approach on eighteen to move 2 up with only two holes remaining. The competitors eventually made the long trek back to the skipped tenth hole to finish the match. There, Microdick Nick finally closed the door and completed one of the greatest comebacks in Shire Cup history. Final Result: Microdick Nick def. Hog Downer after overcoming a four-hole deficit.

Competition Formats

Scramble

4 Matches

6 Holes

Both players hit each shot. The team selects the best ball and both players play from that spot until holed.

Alternate Shot

4 Matches

6 Holes

Partners alternate hitting the same ball until the hole is completed.

Scotch

4 Matches

6 Holes

Both players tee off, select a drive, then alternate shots with one ball until holed.

Bramble

4 Matches

6 Holes

Both players hit drives, choose the best drive, then each player plays their own ball into the hole.

Low / Combined

4 Matches

6 Holes

The lowest individual score and the combined team score are both compared each hole. 1 pt awarded for lowest score, 1 pt for lowest team total. Team can get .5, 1, 1.5 or 2pts per hole

Individuals

8 Matches

6 Holes

Head-to-head singles match play with one point awarded to the winner.

How Points Are Won

Each match is worth 1 point. If a match is tied after 6 holes, each team receives 0.5 points. A total of 28 points are available during the competition, and the team with the most points wins the Shire Cup. IN THE EVENT OF A 14-14 TIE, a sudden death 4 v 4 scramble will take place with 4 players chosen by each captain.
