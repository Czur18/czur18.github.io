Event Summary

The Shire Cup is a Ryder Cup style team competition contested over 36 holes at Wyndhurst Golf Club. Two teams of eight players battle through five team formats and individual match play to determine the annual champion.

A total of 24 points are available throughout the day. The team with the most points at the end of competition claims the Shire Cup.
