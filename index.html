Event Overview

The Shire Cup is a 36-hole Ryder Cup style golf competition featuring team Hog vs team Microdick competing in six different formats:
  • 2v2 Scramble
  • 2v2 Alternate Shot
  • 2v2 Scotch
  • 2v2 Bramble
  • 2v2 Low / Combined
  • Individual Matches

2026 Captains Match Recap

The 2026 Shire Cup captains match between Hog Downer and Microdick Nick was one for the ages.

On a cold Saturday morning at the old Cranwell Resort, now known as Wyndhurst Golf Club, the two captains teed it up in what would become an instant classic.

The match began with matching pars on the opening hole after Nick stuffed a wedge to eight feet but failed to convert the birdie putt. Things quickly turned ugly on the second hole as both players stumbled to sevens, sending the match to the third hole all square.

Disaster struck for Nick on the third after rolling his tee shot and then blasting his third shot into the woods, allowing Hog to take a 1-up lead.

Nick finally responded with a gritty par on six to trim the deficit to two. However, Hog was far from finished. Consecutive pars on seven and eight extended the lead to four holes and appeared to put the match out of reach.

Then came the controversy. As the pair made the turn, eight groups slipped Big E a dollar and skipped directly to the back nine, forcing the captains to jump ahead and completely altering the momentum of the match. When Nick made a par on 12 to cut the lead to 2. On 13, Nick was staring down a crucial ten-footer. After Hog holed a clutch putt, Nick answered by rolling his attempt straight into the center of the cup to maintain the two-hole deficit.

The pivotal moment came at the notorious "Pond Jump" hole. After finding the bunker, Nick produced one of the best shots of the day before pouring in an eight-footer to cut the lead to one.

Standing on the next tee, Nick famously declared: "I've nicked the Hog's main artery."

Moments later, Hog sprayed a tee shot toward the trees but received a lucky kick and managed to stay alive in the hole with a brilliant punch shot and pitch. Unfortunately for Downer, Nick nearly holed his approach for a kick-in birdie, completely erasing what had once been a four-hole deficit. Matching bogeys on sixteen left the match all square. The comeback was complete. On the next hole, a lost ball from Hog allowed Nick to take his first lead of the day at 1 up with three to play. Nick then struck another beautiful approach on eighteen to move 2 up with only two holes remaining. The competitors eventually made the long trek back to the skipped tenth hole to finish the match. There, Microdick Nick finally closed the door and completed one of the greatest comebacks in Shire Cup history.

Final Result: Microdick Nick def. Hog Downer after overcoming a four-hole deficit.

Competition Formats

Scramble

4 Matches

6 Holes

Both players hit each shot. The team selects the best ball and both players play from that spot until holed.

Alternate Shot

4 Matches

6 Holes

Partners alternate hitting the same ball until the hole is completed.

Scotch

4 Matches

6 Holes

Both players tee off, select a drive, then alternate shots with one ball until holed.

Bramble

4 Matches

6 Holes

Both players hit drives, choose the best drive, then each player plays their own ball into the hole.

Low / Combined

4 Matches

6 Holes

The lowest individual score and the combined team score are both compared each hole. 1 pt awarded for lowest score, 1 pt for lowest team total. Team can get .5, 1, 1.5 or 2pts per hole

Individuals

8 Matches

6 Holes

Head-to-head singles match play with one point awarded to the winner.

How Points Are Won

Each match is worth 1 point. If a match is tied after 6 holes, each team receives 0.5 points. A total of 28 points are available during the competition, and the team with the most points wins the Shire Cup. IN THE EVENT OF A 14-14 TIE, a sudden death 4 v 4 scramble will take place with 4 players chosen by each captain.

2026 Shire Cup Preview

The 2026 Shire Cup is shaping up to be the most competitive edition in tournament history. For the first time, Team Hog and Team Microdick will battle across six formats and 28 total points with bragging rights hanging in the balance.

Perhaps the biggest shock of the draft was Carl Zurrin going first overall. Experts, fans, and several unnamed league executives are still trying to understand how that hell that happened. Nevertheless, Carl finds himself leading the charge for Team Microdick.

As always, Ian Roberts and Dan Fuore enter the event as two of the most reliable performers in Shire Cup history. Both players have built reputations for delivering points year after year and will be expected to shoulder plenty of responsibility for their respective teams.

One of the biggest storylines involves first-timer Tim Steis. With no Shire Cup experience, the rookie enters the event as a complete wildcard. Will Tim become an instant legend, or will he spend the day wondering what exactly he signed up for?

Family bragging rights will once again be on the line as brothers Nasty Nate Armstrong and Tricky Ricky Armstrong suit up for Team Hog. The Armstrong duo brings experience, competitive fire, and a healthy amount of trash talk to the roster.

Another family affair features brothers Tyson Besse and Cam Besse. Tyson, a former winning captain, enters with both experience and hardware. Cam returns after an injury absence and will be looking to prove he hasn't lost a step.

Former captains Sam Canfield and Phil Cohen will once again find themselves on opposite sides. Phil's captaincy resulted in a championship victory, a fact he has reportedly reminded everyone of at least once a week ever since.

Meanwhile, James Forgett and Kelsey Williams already have one eye on the future. The pair are scheduled to serve as captains for the 2027 Shire Cup and will undoubtedly begin laying the groundwork for next year's rivalry.

Also enjoying a rare stress-free tournament is former captain James Sena. After suffering defeat at the hands of Colin Benner in a previous edition, Sena catches a break this year with Benner sidelined by injury. Conveniently, Sena insists the timing is purely coincidental.

One lingering question remains unanswered: Will Matt Lynch be miserable?

History says yes. The betting markets say yes. Most of his teammates say yes. Only time will tell.

Hog Downer enters the event determined to prove his captains match collapse was a fluke. A veteran competitor with plenty of confidence and even more opinions, Downer has spent the better part of a year assuring anyone within earshot that Team Hog is destined for greatness. Across the aisle stands Nick Greenleaf, who has fully embraced his role as public enemy number one. Fresh off the greatest comeback the event has ever seen, Nick carries the swagger of a man who believes the cup is already headed home with Team Microdick.

When the dust settles, one captain will have their name etched into Shire Cup history as a champion. The other will spend the entire offseason hearing about it.

With rivalries, family feuds, comeback stories, former champions, future captains, and countless opportunities for public embarrassment, the stage is set for another unforgettable Shire Cup.
